I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Computer Vision at [LIPADE](http://lipade.mi.parisdescartes.fr), in the [SiP](https://sip.mi.parisdescartes.fr/) (Intelligent Systems of Perception) team, under the supervision of [Camille Kurtz](https://camille-kurtz.com/) and [Laurent Wendling](https://helios2.mi.parisdescartes.fr/~lwendlin/) (LIPADE), and [Michaël Clément](https://www.labri.fr/perso/mclement/) ([LaBRI](https://www.labri.fr), Bordeaux INP, Univ. Bordeaux, CNRS).

I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence in 2023.

I am currently working on the integration of spatial information in neural networks. 
My current goals are to allow models to disentangle spatial relations according to reference frames in textual descriptions of images.

My Ph.D. focuses on providing neural networks with novel supervision strategies and architectures that mimic human spatial cognition.
Thesis defense is expected between september and november 2026.
